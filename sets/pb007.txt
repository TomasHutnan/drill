# --- Why software engineering ---

[single] Which definition of software engineering is given by IEEE Std. 610.12-1990?
- The art of writing efficient programs in a high-level language
* The application of a systematic, disciplined, quantifiable approach to the development, operation, and maintenance of software
- The theoretical study of algorithms, computation and information
- The management of programmers within a fixed budget and schedule
> "...that is, the application of engineering to software."

[multi] In the IEEE definition of software engineering, which meanings of the terms are correct?
* Systematic: intentional, repeatable ways of working
* Disciplined: agreed practices followed by teams
* Quantifiable: evidence, measurement, feedback
- Quantifiable: the amount of code produced per developer
- Development, operation, maintenance: only the phases before first delivery
> The last term covers the whole lifecycle.

[single] When and where did the term "software engineering" emerge as a response to late, over-budget and unreliable projects?

- 2001, Agile Manifesto meeting
